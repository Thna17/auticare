Mission & Clinical Context

Hong Thanbratna
Problem & Solution
⚠️ The Challenge in Cambodia
  • Late Diagnosis: Children often screened after age 6 due to stigma and lack of rural access.
  • Language Barrier: Few validated developmental screening tools exist in pure Khmer.
  • Siloed Ecosystem: Parents, doctors, and special education teachers communicate via lost paper slips or unorganized chats.
💡 The AutiCare Solution
  • Pure Khmer Localization: Culturally adapted M-CHAT-R/F with zero parenthetical translation clutter.
  • Connected Circle of Care: Single transactional source of truth linking family, hospital, and school.
  • Ethical Medical Boundary: Prominent clinical disclaimers connecting families directly to licensed pediatricians.
